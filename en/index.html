Common questions

Smart replenishment
in practice.

The questions we hear most from those who want to stop restocking by guesswork.

How does Listio make "automatic" replenishment without sales integration?

Listio uses what you control with 100% certainty: real stock. At each checklist, it compares with the previous one, understands what went out, and transforms that into a replenishment suggestion — without needing heavy ERP software.

Does the purchase suggestion come with quantities?

Yes. The replenishment list generates suggested quantities based on your count and the pace of outflow between periods. You can adjust before sending the order.

Does this replace my buying spreadsheet?

In practice, yes. Instead of "building" an order manually, you do the checklist and get the replenishment ready. Many keep their sales system and use Listio exclusively for replenishment.

Will I forget fewer items in the order?

Yes — because the checklist is the complete list. You count the entire stock and the replenishment comes from that, reducing the risk of "forgetting" a product by depending on search.

Do I need to enter a credit card?

No. You can start for free, test the suggested replenishment in your routine, and only then decide.
